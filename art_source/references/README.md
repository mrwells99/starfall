## Archive scope and provenance

- Ember, Luminary, Fulcrum, Vanguard and Outlaw art was already preserved here when checked. The available local generated-image folder contained Outlaw, Null and an unrelated ability icon; no additional class sheet was found there.
- The early Vanguard illustration was copied from `C:/Users/aidan/Downloads/ChatGPT_Image_Sep_8_2026_01_37_56_PM.png`. It is historical art, not the current model reference.
- Null v1 was generated using the built-in image generation tool and copied into this folder. Its subtitle, quotation, costume and blade details, and six abilities are proposals. The unique eternal identity, twin sentient blades and warp/spacetime power source come from the user.
- Exact original generation prompts for Ember, Luminary, Fulcrum and Vanguard were not found in the inspected reference archive. Their artwork, embedded sheet information and existing project design notes are preserved; do not invent historical prompts.
- Current gameplay kit documentation is in [CLASS_ABILITIES.md](../../docs/CLASS_ABILITIES.md). Concept art may depict older or unimplemented abilities.
