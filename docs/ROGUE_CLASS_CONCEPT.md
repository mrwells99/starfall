# Rogue class: Null

Updated September 11, 2026. This user-established direction supersedes the earlier Hollowkin lore.

## Established identity

**Null** is a male, non-human, classic rogue who holds two sentient blades. He is the only one of his kind ever to exist and has existed for as long as time itself. He is the only class that does not transmute cosmic energy for attacks: he draws directly from the warp and the fabric of spacetime.

Retain the Hollowkin living-shadow aesthetic only. Discard the species/kin, deceased-owner origin, and stolen names and memories lore.

## Direction for future class work
