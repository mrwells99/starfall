# Outlaw — Starforged Drifter

Playable first pass, September 9, 2026. Built from the approved Outlaw concept and Starfall Model Forge v2. The robot wears blackened steel, brass, a weathered duster and cowboy hat, with amber optics, a blue celestial revolver and a crimson-etched Bowie knife. The owner requested the knife at **150% of its first-pass size** and a real closed-hand grip. It is held across the left palm in a reverse grip, with the blade clear of the torso; the right hand holds the revolver.

**Follow-up network audit:** Roll now has its own local visual clock, including the slower animation and crouched recovery after physical travel ends. The lasso pose layers coexist with the shared network-motion smoothing; Backflip retains its interpolated visual clock. The 20 Hz snapshot regression checks that Roll advances without held or reversed frames across the travel/recovery boundary and stops when recovery ends.

Coin Toss advances along its existing arc between packets with a visual terrain clamp. Gun/knife timers already advance locally. The shared animator also separates gait speed from network corrections and smooths the remote jump-arm input. Roll distance/duration, coin collision/ricochet validation and combat state remain authoritative. Details and testing are in [Technical Architecture](TECHNICAL_ARCHITECTURE.md#animation-timing-across-network-updates--september-10-audit).

## Kit

**Current balance, version 0.11.1:** Severe deals rounded 20% current HP plus its existing 20/s bleed for 5s, and slows by 60% for 6s. Affected characters use walking/strafe animation at 40% movement speed; this does not add another forced-walking speed penalty. The strongest slow applies. Roll grants 25% speed for 5s and one instant Severe for 1.5s even when a wall stops it at zero distance. Airborne Backflip reduces damage by 50%; stronger existing protection takes precedence. Trickshot deals 180. Boot Kick interrupts within 3m, locks spells for 4s and has a 12s cooldown, off GCD. Every class gains the 120s stun-breaking Trinket. Both client and server need this version.

Existing class slots stay fixed. Boot Kick is kit index 12 (Shift+6), Trinket is shared index 14 (Ctrl+1); unavailable indices 10 and 13 stay hidden. Saved layouts preserve existing bindings and add missing abilities without overwriting key conflicts. In-game Outlaw descriptions are condensed; range, cast, cooldown and off-GCD metadata remain visible.

The final Deadeye clarification supersedes the earlier front-facing answer: everyone is acquired, and final sight range/terrain decide hits. Backflip itself never deals damage or awards resources; the separate successful Trickshot does. Both combo windows are single-use. All authoritative combat state resets at round and duel boundaries and is included in network snapshots. Shared shields, mitigation and world-duel permissions still apply.

Backflip can now begin during an ordinary jump's ascent, apex or descent. It starts at the current airborne position with the normal Backflip impulse, cooldown, combo opportunity and CC immunity. Roll's instant Severe window is now 1.5 seconds, consumed on successful Severe use.

Lasso is appended at kit index 11; the retired Aim Test at index 10 stays hidden, preserving saved layouts. Cooldown starts on successful release; landing or cancellation during windup grants no stack and spends no cooldown. Rope speed is 70m/s and pull speed is 32m/s. A minimum 0.12s approach presentation completes the kick at short range. Knockback travels over 0.18s, with a 0.38m-high, approximately 0.44s retreat for Outlaw. The existing GCD applies. These unspecified range/cooldown/distance values are initial tuning choices. Lasso adds no damage.

The travel stun and impact knockdown share one DR application (full/half/quarter/immune). Cleansing that stun prevents reapplication on impact. Another caster's stun is never cleared by Lasso cleanup. Walls, target loss, death and a bounded travel timeout safely end an unfinished pull without awarding a stack. Grounded Lasso remains susceptible to hard control, root and forced displacement. When begun during Backflip, Lasso preserves CC/displacement immunity through airborne windup, rope flight, pull and rebound; immunity ends on landing or completion. The airborne Lasso buff identifies that protection without promising another Trickshot opportunity after rope release. The 1.5s knockdown includes the victim's fall and get-up animation; Outlaw recovers sooner.

Lasso handles height changes with a short, server-chosen route swept by the full movement capsule. It approaches at the target's foot height and, when required, lifts just enough to clear the departure/arrival ledge before crossing and descending. It does not phase through a wall or low ceiling; missing clearance safely cancels. The route is replicated for prediction and rechecked if the target moves. Uphill/downhill terraces, nearly vertical airborne descent, safe cancellation, free movement afterward and networked ledge travel are covered by regression checks. Scoped evidence: `artifacts/outlaw-lasso-ledges/`.

Recovery pacing: Outlaw's post-dropkick backflip plays another 20% slower than the previous 0.35s version, taking 0.4375s (about 0.44s). The target's fall and get-up each play approximately 14% slower, occupying 35% and 49% of the knockdown respectively, with a 16% grounded hold. At full stun duration this is 0.525s falling, 0.24s grounded and 0.735s rising. The same fractions follow stun diminishing returns and end with control returning. Ordinary Roll and Backflip retain their accepted timing. Scoped rollback files and review evidence are under `artifacts/outlaw-lasso-recovery/`, with the additional Outlaw-only slowdown under `artifacts/outlaw-lasso-recovery-r2/`.

Airborne Lasso slows captured world-space horizontal velocity to 25% once. Descent uses gravity 4m/s² capped at 1.5m/s; upward motion retains normal gravity 20m/s². Both horizontal drift and slow descent continue while the rope travels, instead of freezing the caster. The original velocity is retained with normal gravity advancing in parallel, and restored immediately if the windup is cancelled, completion validation fails, or the rope fails. This restores the appropriate falling velocity rather than replaying an old upward launch. Terrain contacts remove blocked momentum; landing, death and newer displacement take precedence. Momentum state travels in authoritative snapshots and is replayed with normal movement prediction. Current checks and rollback copies are under `artifacts/outlaw-lasso-momentum/`.

Presentation is a separate runtime action layer over unchanged Forge v2 models, materials, base clips and native hitbox dimensions. A separate aimed-trace expansion now doubles horizontal width/depth and increases height by 25%, without changing the movement capsule or rig anatomy. `tools/build_lasso_motion.gd` samples the supplied Quaternius library's Death01, Roll and Spell_Simple_Enter into a portable, shared pose resource. The dropkick adds articulated bone aiming; the brief rebound reverses native Roll, and get-up reverses the native fall because this library has no dedicated dropkick or get-up. Both visible characters and mesh-free server rigs run the same layer. Reusable wrist-rope/loop effects and a cowboy lasso icon complete the first pass. Review images, frame timings, logs and scoped rollback records are under `artifacts/outlaw-lasso/`.

Defense Detonation firing is now active. The server reserves the burst at the first valid shot; later shots use their own camera aim and cannot exceed that reservation or fire faster than 0.13s apart. Misses still spend stacks, while a new stack earned during a burst remains for the next one. Terrain and friendly/protected bodies stop the damage ray. Cancelling aim, losing focus, hard CC, casting another ability or starting Roll/Backflip cancels unsent/remaining shots without restoring already committed stacks. The camera utility itself remains usable at zero stacks and during the GCD, but cannot fire then. See [OUTLAW_AIM_TEST.md](OUTLAW_AIM_TEST.md) for camera validation, recoil and network checks.

Deadeye's refund covers manual cancellation, applicable crowd control, forced movement that cancels a cast, death and premature duel cleanup. A completed cast retains its cooldown even if everyone escapes range or hides behind cover. An ineffective kick or a root alone neither interrupts nor refunds Deadeye. Coin Toss captures movement once: sideways, backward and airborne velocity contribute in world space; later turning, stopping or reversing cannot steer the coin. The original 1.8s opportunity and swept terrain check remain in place. Severe's 3.3m reach applies to normal and instant Roll follow-ups and is checked again at cast completion.

September 10 Backflip tuning: the first accepted adjustment doubled backward travel and reduced height by 15%. The latest adjustment reduces that accepted height another 7% and increases that accepted travel another 20%. Vertical launch speed is `12 * sqrt(0.85 * 0.93)`; backward speed is `3 * 2 * 1.2 / sqrt(0.85 * 0.93)`, compensating for the shorter airtime. At 60 physics ticks/s, the latest change measures 7.159→8.638m of travel and 3.009→2.797m of rise; at 30 ticks/s, rise measures 2.919→2.708m. Measured airtime is 1.067s. Discrete gravity and collision steps account for small differences from ideal percentages. Heading, terrain collision, landing, immunity and single-use Trickshot rules remain unchanged.

The actual flip now starts near takeoff: reversed Roll begins at 60% of the source clip (0.9s), skipping the forward roll's recovery section, and reaches source time zero at the computed landing time. Backflip's entry blend uses 0.08s for both limbs and torso; ordinary Roll retains its existing 0.08/0.16s blend. The body turns about 29 degrees from its starting pose by 0.1s at 30/60 FPS, with a smooth first frame; 144 FPS behaves consistently. Source clips, anatomy, equipment and ordinary jump smoothing are unchanged.

A local receipt counter on Combatant identifies new reports; neither the counter nor the visual clock is serialized. Offline/authoritative animation retains the exact gameplay timer. New movement revisions reset phase; late visibility starts at the reported phase; the clock clamps at the final pose during outages. Authoritative landing, death or interruption stops the special presentation. No source animation, launch physics, combo eligibility, immunity, damage, Roll timing or network payload changed.
