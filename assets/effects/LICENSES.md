# Fulcrum sword

`fulcrum_dark_sword.obj` is an adapted Claymore from [Quaternius Medieval Weapons](https://quaternius.com/packs/medievalweapons.html), released under **CC0 1.0**. Adaptation normalizes its reach and broadens the blade for a spectral effect. The original pack and four other downloaded sword alternatives remain in the local source library.

The sword is an independent reusable model, not extracted Elden Ring content. Elden Ring spell footage supplied motion/scale reference only.
